Kafae Boran · "ancient coffee" in Thai

A little Thailand on Montilla Boulevard

Strong Thai-style coffee, oat-based matcha, Tom Yam and Pad Thai, mango sticky rice, and cocktails like the Songkran and Phaya Naga. Come for a slow coffee in the morning or stay for dinner and drinks with the barkada.
